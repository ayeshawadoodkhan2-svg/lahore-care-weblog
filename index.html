RETHINKING COMMUNITY CARE IN LAHORE

posted on: oct 14, 2026

whenever people at school or in town bring up charity work, it's usually about raising big funds or throwing a one-off event. but honestly, that always felt a bit disconnected to me.

when you look at children living in local shelters, they don't just need people showing up once a year to drop off boxes and take photos. what actually matters is consistency. just being present, helping with schoolwork, or running art sessions on weekends makes a huge difference in making kids feel supported and valued in their own city.

LOCAL SPOTLIGHT: LAHORE SHELTERS

case study notes

i wanted to highlight two long-standing places in lahore that show how structured care can actually work when done right:

LOCATION #1

SOS Children's Village Lahore

Address: Ferozepur Road, Lahore

sos village on ferozepur road operates using a family-house model where kids live together in small homes with a house mother. they focus on school, stability, and growing up in an actual neighborhood setup.

LOCATION #2

Edhi Home Lahore

Address: Muslim Block, Allama Iqbal Town, Lahore

located in allama iqbal town, the edhi center provides immediate shelter, food, healthcare, and schooling for children. it shows the power of grassroots relief right inside our community.

3 SIMPLE WAYS TO ACTUALLY HELP

no pressure guide

you don't need a huge budget to start getting involved. here are three realistic things youth in lahore can do:

01.
weekend tutoring: helping out with basic homework, reading storybooks, or explaining math concepts a couple hours a month.
02.
creative workshops: bringing over paint, sketchbooks, or sports gear to run casual afternoon activities.
03.
neighborhood drives: gathering winter jackets, stationery supplies, and books from friends and family.

FINAL THOUGHTS

at the end of the day, this page is just about getting people my age to think differently about local orphanages. if we start treating local care as a normal part of our community responsibility, lahore becomes a better place for everyone.
